03 Systems integration · Research workflow

Zotero ↔ NotebookLM

Built for Julia Turc, founder of Storia AI: a bidirectional workflow connecting a research library in Zotero with Google NotebookLM.

System
Zotero plugin, browser extension, local bridge
Output
Bidirectional research workflow
  • Three-part bridge: plugin, authenticated browser context, local IPC
  • Forward sync of source files and metadata into NotebookLM
  • Reverse sync of generated notes back into the research library
